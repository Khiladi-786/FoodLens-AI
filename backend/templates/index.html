How FoodLens AI Works

Four seamless steps from food label capture to personalized ingredient intelligence.

01

SCAN

Upload or capture a food label using your mobile or desktop camera.

02

EXTRACT

Optical Character Recognition (OCR) parses and tokenizes raw ingredient lists.

03

ANALYZE

Matches detected ingredients against your stored allergy, health, and diet profile.

04

RECOMMEND

Discover suitable ingredient alternatives based on your food profile.
